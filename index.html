HOW TO PLAY & MECHANICS

🕹️ BASIC CONTROLS
  • A / D or ◄ / ►: Run Left & Right
  • SPACE or W: Jump (Hold for higher jump)
  • E or K: Swing stick to break crates
  • ESC or P: Pause / Return to Menu
💥 COMBAT & ABILITIES
  • HEAD STOMP: Jump on top of Ghost & Spider heads to squash them instantly and bounce!
  • STICK GROUND SLAM [F or J]: Slam your stick into the ground for a MASSIVE AoE shockwave that obliterates nearby enemies! (10s Cooldown)
🗝️ CRATES & ESCAPE
  • Smash wooden crates to find the Golden Temple Key.
  • Once found, bring the Key to the glowing Exit Portal to clear the level!
  • Each level gets harder with faster monsters and trickier gaps.
👑 BRAINROT BOSSES & HEALING
  • Every 3 Levels: Face Brainrot Bosses with Undertale bullet-hell soundwaves and Cuphead rush attacks!
  • ★ REWARD: After defeating the boss every 3 levels, you gain back 3 Health!
